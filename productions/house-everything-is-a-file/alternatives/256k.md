# 256K

**Logline.** ALIX doubts a club the size of a microcontroller. XILA runs the door, keeps the room dancing through one spectacular wipeout, and invites you in through your browser.

ALIX gets the long question. XILA gets four clipped syllables and one held word. The first four syllables strike the kick, then “Plenty” gets the bass. Every return reveals more choreography inside the same footprint. At the peak, the missing kick makes the joke before the next kick supplies the answer. Treat the whole picture as a music video for a venue with a very unbothered doorman.

## Song and picture

## Art direction

Hot offset-print colors over a midnight club plan, with mint acrylic, raised aprons and visible beveled stage props. Key lighting identifies the active path rather than washing the entire room. Lemon is the lead guest and the hook color. Violet is the ensemble and the overconfident soloist. Coral is reserved for the failure. Unchanging corners sell the scale joke. Four motifs tie the rooms together: copper contact pads, a stair-step path, wedge-shaped light segments and the club's mint rail.

| Color  | Hex       | Use                                    |
| ------ | --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Night  | `#0D0921` | Venue exterior, final field            |
| Plum   | `#211546` | Floor, structure                       |
| Violet | `#AE86FF` | Waiting guests and dance-floor soloist |
| Mint   | `#58F6E0` | Rail, working path, active turnstile   |
| Lemon  | `#E7FC57` | Marquee, lead guest                    |
| Coral  | `#FF7867` | Trap and failed tile                   |
| Chalk  | `#F5F1FF` | Small physical lettering               |

Use pinned Montserrat throughout, 900 for big display, 700–800 for signage. The narrow marquee fascia and stacked heavy letters give the club its condensed silhouette within the one family. Use tabular numerals when tracking the hook. Keep intentional narrative signage at least 34 px tall at 1080p. Genuine UI retains its recorded typography and is framed for the visible stroke. The branded finish uses `Logo({lockup: true})`, with the official white variant flat and unaltered over the dark field and at least one-quarter symbol width of clear space.
