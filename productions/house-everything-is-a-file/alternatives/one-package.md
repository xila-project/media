# One Package

A signed app sleeve headlines a three-room progressive house tour that starts at a GPIO pin and ends in the viewer’s own browser tab.

**Hook, XILA** — “Same app. Every room.”

It takes one bar to learn the hook. The next bar leaves space to answer it. ALIX plays the self-important tour manager, budgeting for a light show and then mistaking one app trap for the end of the set. XILA has two jobs: sing the hook and keep the kick going. The floor plan links the rooms, sets up the jokes and carries the proof. On the last chorus the audience gets a destination they can open.

The tiny Pin Room is the first stop on **Xila’s** tour. The signed sleeve first appears outside the Pin Room during the pull-back and travels only between the native and browser floors. Whenever the hook plays, its lettering belongs to those two floors and the qualifier **“The same bundle seeds native and browser images.”** holds in the plan’s lower margin. The separated GPIO stage has no package route.

The preserved beat is a literal line with one peak per kick.
