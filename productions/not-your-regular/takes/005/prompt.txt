Fast, driving, bright 130 BPM nu-disco electropop in C minor with a relative-major chorus lift: punchy short four-on-the-floor kick, crisp dry claps on 2 and 4, busy 16th-note hats, a bouncy staccato octave synth bass and a catchy formant-shifted vocal-chop earworm motif, high energy from the first bar. The lead is a young, insolent, playful gen-Z female pop singer with a flirty undertone who sings fast, catchy melodic lines with heavy, glossy, clearly audible auto-tune on every sung phrase, light vocal fry and breathy head-voice choruses with stacked harmonies; this is sung pop, never rap or talk-rap. A warm, bone-dry, deadpan low male baritone only speaks short interjections with zero reverb, and the band stops dead for his punchlines. Glossy commercial pop mix with airy vocal sparkle, tight low end and click micro-percussion; no shouting or belting.
