Upbeat 130 BPM nu-disco electropop comedy duet in a minor key with a straight, bouncy four-on-the-floor groove, crisp dry handclaps on 2 and 4 and a tight rubbery octave synth bass. Verses strip back to punchy drums, muted plucky synths and plenty of dry negative space; choruses bloom with bright, wide stereo synth-pop chords and a playful pitched synth-pluck earworm motif. A bright, expressive medium-range male tenor lead delivers nimble, smirky talk-sung verses that lift into light, effortless, melodic pop choruses with clean harmonies, never shouted or belted, against a cool, ultra-dry, close-miked deadpan assistant voice with a clearly different timbre that answers flatly without singing. Glossy radio-ready pop mix with airy high-end sparkle, tight mono low end, tactile click micro-percussion and sharp one-beat stop-time silences for comic timing.
