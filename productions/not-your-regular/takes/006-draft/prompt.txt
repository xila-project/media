Euphoric 130 BPM electropop in A minor, chords changing every two beats with a major-V lift. Simple tight groove: short four-on-the-floor kick, dry claps on 2 and 4, straight 8th-note hats, no rolling or 16th-note bass. The bass sits out in the verses and saves its full weight for the drop. A young, insolent, flirty gen-Z female lead chants the verses on one repeated note with hard robotic auto-tune, deadpan and catchy, never rap; her melody only blooms on the hook, glossy and stacked. A deep, bone-dry male voice only speaks in total silences and never sings. Hard stop-times: the whole band drops out for spoken lines, then slams back. Clean, bright mix with big dynamic contrast between quiet verses and loud hooks; no shouting, no belting.
