Bratty, catchy English electropop dance-pop duet at 128 BPM: four-on-the-floor kick, bouncy synth-bass riff, side-chained synth chords, tight claps and a cheeky kazoo-like lead synth, crisp modern production. Two lead voices trade lines rapid-fire with every syllable intelligible: a playful, cocky young male pop vocalist in a medium range (light baritone, neither deep nor high) and a deadpan, lightly vocoded robotic voice; hooks are shouted gang chants. Energy arc: filtered spoken-sung intro opening into the first drop, dead stop-time breaks with a clock tick, a cheesy half-time infomercial bridge with cash-register hits, a glitchy breakdown that cuts to silence, the biggest final chorus and a pure chant drop with a hard stop, then dry close-mic whispers over one soft synth chord.
