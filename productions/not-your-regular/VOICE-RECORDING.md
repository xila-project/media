# Maintainer voice recording

The ALIX lead in "Not Your Regular" is generated by ElevenLabs Music with a stand-in male voice (medium range, light baritone). The plan is to convert that isolated stem to the maintainer's Instant Voice Clone with the Voice Changer (`eleven_multilingual_sts_v2`). Singing through the Voice Changer is not documented, so the result needs a listening test before it is adopted. If the conversion sounds wrong, the fallback is for the maintainer to perform the ALIX lines over the instrumental and to mix that recording in directly.

## What to record

Record about **2 minutes** in English as **one file**, using the 3 parts below. A phone is fine if the room is quiet.

- **Setup.** Hold the mic about 20 cm away. Use a room with soft furnishings (or a wardrobe), with no music, fan or echo.
- **File.** Use WAV or high-quality m4a. Do not apply noise reduction or other effects.
- **Level.** Speak with your normal energy; the louder parts must not distort.

1. **Natural speech (~45 s).** Read any English paragraph calmly, for example the Xila README.
2. **Show voice (~45 s).** Use the energy of the song: cheeky, smiling, a bit of a TV-host voice. Read the ALIX lines in `SONG.md` (Intro, Verse 1, Bridge "Order now!…").
3. **Chant / half-sung (~30 s).** Shout and sing over a beat in your head:
   - "Not your regular! O-S!"
   - "Board! Desk! Tab!"
   - "I cleared a desk, I hired a van, I bought a giant cooling fan!"

   Hitting the right notes does not matter. The goal is to give the clone your voice in that register.
